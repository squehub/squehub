# Community conduct

SqueHub welcomes thoughtful technical discussion and contributions. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and make room for people with different experience and backgrounds. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and deliberate disclosure of someone else's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

These expectations apply to SqueHub issues, pull requests, and other project-hosted discussions. If a discussion becomes harmful, maintainers may moderate it or limit participation. To report a conduct concern privately, email **hello@squehub.com** with enough context to identify the discussion. Do not include unrelated personal data.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting path](SECURITY.md).
