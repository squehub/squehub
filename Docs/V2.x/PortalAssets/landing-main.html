OPERATE WITH INTENT 06 / 08

Inspect. Preview.
Then decide.

Good decisions need a clear picture of the current application. SqueHub provides focused checks and reviewable plans before you take the next step.

01 / CHECK THE ENVIRONMENT
$ php squehub doctor

See what needs attention.

Doctor reports safe outcomes for configured dependencies and selected services. It inspects; it does not repair or deploy.

Read Health & Doctor ↗
02 / REVIEW A CHANGE
$ php squehub make:controller Admin/UserController --preview

Know what will change.

Preview a generator plan, review conflicts and risk, then apply deliberately through its own command.
